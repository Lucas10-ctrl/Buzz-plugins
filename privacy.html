Social Poster privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Social Poster is a tool that Intelliminds (intelliminds.ai) uses to schedule and publish posts to its own social media accounts. It runs only on our own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What it stores

When we connect one of our accounts, Social Poster stores the account's name, picture and the sign-in token the network gives it. It also stores the posts, pictures and videos we write. All of this is kept in a file on our own computer. Nothing is sent to any server run by us or anyone else.

What it does with it

Social Poster uses the token only to publish the posts we schedule and to read back the link to each published post. It does not read other people's data, sell data, show ads or share data with anyone.

Removing data

Removing an account in Social Poster deletes its token from our computer. You can also remove Social Poster's access at any time in the network's own settings.
