Social Poster terms of service

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Social Poster is a tool that Intelliminds (intelliminds.ai) uses to schedule and publish posts to its own social media accounts. It runs on our own computer and is not offered to the public.

Only people who manage Intelliminds' accounts use it. Everyone who uses it must follow the rules of each social network it posts to. Every post is written or approved by a person at Intelliminds before it goes out.

The software is provided as is, without any promise that it will always work. The code is open source: https://github.com/Lucas10-ctrl/Buzz-plugins/tree/main/social-poster
